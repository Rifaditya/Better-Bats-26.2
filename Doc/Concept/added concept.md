# Backlog Items (Better Bats)

1. **Expanded Roost Hanging**: Bats can roost upside down under stalactites (dripstone), chains, fences, and hanging lanterns.
2. **Physical Guano Item Harvest**: Optional GameRule (`better-bats:bat_drop_guano_item`) to drop Bone Meal items when resting over non-farmland blocks.
3. **Predator Avoidance**: Bats scatter in panic when Cats, Ocelots, or Phantoms approach within 10 blocks.
